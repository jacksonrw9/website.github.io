Close the gap between your instruments and LabWare

Operations creates the sampling plan, LabWare manages it, and instruments produce the data. Nothing connects the three, even though GxP environments require proof that the right instrument produced each result, the data arrived intact, and it reached the correct sample tables.

LabWare Loop, powered by Phizzle, closes the gap: instruments connect directly to your LabWare sampling workflows, removing manual transfers for your lab and giving QA traceable data with full context, from sampling plan to instrument to results table.
